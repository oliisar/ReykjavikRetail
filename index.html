Who We Are

Iceland's Most
Trusted Retail
Importer

Founded in the heart of Reykjavík, we have spent over two decades building trade corridors between Iceland and the world. From fresh European foodstuffs to premium household goods, every product we import meets our stringent quality benchmarks before it reaches any shelf in Iceland.

Beyond importing and distribution, we are proud to serve as trusted advisors to some of Iceland's most respected retail businesses — guiding strategy, supplier negotiations, and category management.
